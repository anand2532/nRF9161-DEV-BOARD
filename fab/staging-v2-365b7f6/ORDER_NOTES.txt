ORDER NOTES: nRF9161 v2 UART modem module @ 365b7f6 (prototype)

Order the PANEL: nRF9161-v2-panel-365b7f6.zip, 2×2, 118.10×93.10 mm, mouse-bite tabs, 6 mm frame, 3 fiducials per side, 4 × 2 mm tooling holes. Gate RED (2 via-in-pad items). Do not order until docs/FAB_READY_CHECKLIST_V2.md is GREEN apart from the JLC quote.

Drill scheme (CONFIRMED by Hardware PM, 2026-10-13): fill every 0.20 mm hole.

A. PCB fabrication
1. 4-layer, 1.6 mm FR4, ENIG. Stackup per docs/FAB_STACKUP_NOTES.md and the .gbrjob: F.Cu 35 µm / 0.12 mm / In1 35 µm / 1.2 mm / In2 35 µm / 0.12 mm / B.Cu 35 µm.
2. Via filling: epoxy filled and copper capped (POFV / IPC-4761 Type VII). Fill every 0.20 mm hole and every 0.25 mm hole: 20 vias per board, 80 per panel.
   - 0.20 mm ×11 per board: 7 in U1 GND pads 21, 27, 36, 52, 56, 60 and 98, plus 4 GND vias not in pads at (21.5, 18.95), (21.5, 20.05), (22.78, 22.4) and (18.78, 19.45).
   - 0.25 mm ×9 per board: all in U1 pad 103.
   - These are the only 0.20/0.25 tools in the PTH drill file. No other via is filled; all 0.30 mm vias are tented.
   - POFV is charged on 4-layer boards (https://jlcpcb.com/news/free-via-in-pad-6-20-layer-pcbs-pofv). JLC doesn't publish the price, so the cost is unverified until quoted.
3. Caps planarized flush or slightly recessed, never proud: recess ≤50 µm OK, protrusion ≤+25 µm. JLC's published limit is ±50 µm: https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-via-covering. This applies to both sides.
4. Hole plating: JLC standard for the prototype.
5. Panel: mouse-bite tabs (0.6 mm holes). Deliver as a panel with rails.

B. Assembly (Standard PCBA, double-sided)
1. Standard PCBA. Economic is single-sided only: https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities
2. Parts per board: 51 top, 16 bottom (67 assembled).
   - Bottom: 8 × 0402 C, 2 × 0402 R, Q1 SOT-23, U2 SOT-23-5, U7/U8 SOT-23-6, U5/U6 VSSOP-8.
   - U4 and C33–C38 are now on top.
3. Stencils: Top + Bottom on separate stencils (a JLC option: https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-stencil-manufacturing).
   - Top 0.10 mm (U1 LGA, U4 USON 0.4 mm pitch, 0201s).
   - Bottom 0.10 mm (0402/SOT/VSSOP 0.5 mm). 0.10 and 0.12 mm are standard thicknesses at no extra cost (same page).
4. U1 (nRF9161 LGA) paste at 70–85% of pad area per Nordic nWP054: https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/nwp_054/page/wp/nwp_054/footprint.html. The paste Gerber is 1:1 with copper; please apply the reduction.
5. Reflow order: bottom side first, top side (U1) last. Rechecked at 365b7f6: the bottom is the lighter side (16 parts, 30.1 mm² of pads, no BGA/LGA/QFN) against the top (51 parts, 167.1 mm²). JLC assembles the side with fewer parts first: https://jlcpcb.com/blog/files-needed-for-pcb-assembly
6. First-article X-ray of U1 on all pads, including the 16 via-in-pad locations (GND pads 21, 27, 36, 52, 56, 60, 98 and pad 103). Report voiding >25% or any open joint.
7. JP2 (SIM_VCC, bottom): bridge with solder by hand after reflow. It has no paste openings (checked on B.Paste), so the stencil won't bridge it. Without the bridge the SIM is unpowered. JP1 no longer exists.
8. J20 (1×10 THT header, top): wave/selective solder with the bottom SMT parts protected by a fixture: https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-fixtures
