ORDER NOTES: nRF9161 v2 UART modem module @ d89dc88 (prototype)

Order the PANEL: nRF9161-v2-panel-d89dc88.zip, 2×2, 118.10×93.10 mm, mouse-bite tabs, 6 mm frame, 3 fiducials per side, 4 × 2 mm tooling holes. Gate RED. Do not order until the red items in docs/FAB_READY_CHECKLIST_V2.md are fixed and the JLC quote questions are answered.

Drill scheme: option (a), fill the 4 extra 0.20 mm GND vias near U4. PENDING HARDWARE PM CONFIRMATION. If Hardware PM picks (b), Layout changes those 4 vias to a new drill, and A2 below goes back to 16 vias (7 × 0.20 + 9 × 0.25).

A. PCB fabrication
1. 4-layer, 1.6 mm FR4, ENIG. Stackup per docs/FAB_STACKUP_NOTES.md and the .gbrjob: F.Cu 35 µm / 0.12 mm / In1 35 µm / 1.2 mm / In2 35 µm / 0.12 mm / B.Cu 35 µm.
2. Via filling, epoxy filled and copper capped (POFV / IPC-4761 Type VII), exactly 20 vias per board (80 per panel):
   - every 0.20 mm hole (×11 per board): 7 in U1 GND pads, plus 4 GND vias next to U4/C33/C37 on the bottom (not in pads)
   - every 0.25 mm hole (×9 per board), in U1 pad 103
   - These are the only 0.20/0.25 tools in the PTH drill file. The 16 U1 vias are also marked on the VIPPO layer Gerber; the 4 U4-area vias are found by drill size only.
   - No other via is filled; all other vias are tented.
   - POFV is charged on 4-layer boards (https://jlcpcb.com/news/free-via-in-pad-6-20-layer-pcbs-pofv). JLC doesn't publish the price, so the cost is unverified until quoted.
3. Caps planarized flush or slightly recessed, never proud: recess ≤50 µm OK, protrusion ≤+25 µm. JLC's published limit is ±50 µm: https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-via-covering. This also applies to the 4 bottom-side caps, which sit 0.05 mm from U4's GND pad and 0.25 mm from U4's signal pads. Mask stays over them (tented).
4. Hole plating: JLC standard for the prototype.
5. Panel: mouse-bite tabs (0.6 mm holes). Deliver as a panel with rails.

B. Assembly (Standard PCBA, double-sided)
1. Standard PCBA. Economic is single-sided only: https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-assembly-capabilities
2. Stencils: Top + Bottom on separate stencils (a JLC option: https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-stencil-manufacturing). Top 0.10 mm for U1. Bottom 0.10 mm, which suits the 0201 parts and U4's 0.4 mm pitch. Both are standard thicknesses at no extra cost (same page).
3. U1 (nRF9161 LGA) paste at 70–85% of pad area per Nordic nWP054: https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/nwp_054/page/wp/nwp_054/footprint.html. The paste Gerber is 1:1 with copper; please apply the reduction.
4. Reflow order: bottom side first, top side (U1) last, so U1 is reflowed once. The bottom has 23 parts and the top 44; JLC assembles the side with fewer parts first: https://jlcpcb.com/blog/files-needed-for-pcb-assembly
5. First-article X-ray of U1 on all pads, including the 16 via-in-pad locations (GND pads 21, 27, 36, 52, 56, 60, 98 and pad 103). Report voiding >25% or any open joint.
6. JP2 (SIM_VCC, bottom): bridge with solder by hand after reflow. It has no paste openings (checked on B.Paste), so the stencil won't bridge it. Without the bridge the SIM is unpowered. JP1 no longer exists in v2.
7. J20 (1×10 THT header, top): wave/selective solder with the bottom SMT parts protected by a fixture: https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/pcb-assembly-fixtures
